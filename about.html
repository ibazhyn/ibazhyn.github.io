About

I design software for people whose job is already hard enough.

I am a senior product designer. For more than ten years I have designed mobile apps, SaaS products, dashboards and admin tools — screens that dispatchers, drivers and project teams use to get their work done.

Since 2020 I have worked on one ride-hailing and logistics ecosystem: passenger apps, driver apps, dispatcher CRM, reporting dashboards and admin workflows. I start with the flows and the information architecture, together with the developers, and only then draw the high-fidelity UI.

I also use OpenAI Codex and Claude Code to take designs into code: landing pages, product-site prototypes and admin concepts. I review the HTML, CSS and JavaScript they produce and write instructions developers can build from.

Working style

How I work with product and engineering

  • I write the flows and the information architecture before the screens, and review them with the engineer who will build them.
  • I bring the two options I did not choose to design review, with what each would have cost.
  • I name what I owned, what the team decided together, and what sat outside design scope.
  • I sit with the people who will use an operations tool before proposing anything for it.
  • I specify edge states — empty, partial, denied, stale, offline — in the same file as the happy path, not in a follow-up ticket.
  • I treat handoff as part of the design: decisions written as implementation instructions, not a picture to copy.

AI-assisted development

Where AI sits in the process

I work with OpenAI Codex and Claude Code, but I stay responsible for the result: I check the states, the layout and the accessibility myself, and I don't show a prototype that hides the difficult cases.
